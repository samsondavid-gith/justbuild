Build the shop front.

Session 4 · 90 min  |  Model: Claude-style app builder for the page + a light copy model for the words

Your app is the shop; the website is the front door a stranger trusts in five seconds. One page, one clear button, that opens your app.

Prompt - one-page website
Build me a single-page, mobile-first website for [my business]. A stranger must understand what it is in FIVE seconds. Structure, top to bottom: - A headline that names THEIR problem, not my brand [their pain in their words]. - One line on the outcome I give them (benefit, not features). - One real photo area. - 2-3 trust signals [e.g. "made fresh daily", a real name, a phone number]. - ONE big button: "[action, e.g. Order now]" that links to my live app: [paste your app link]. - A one-tap way to contact me [WhatsApp / phone]. One accent colour, clean and calm, no lorem ipsum - use my real words. Make it a link I can share.
Prompt - the words (run first if stuck on copy)
Write the words for a one-page site for [my business], aimed at [my named person from ideation]. Give me: a headline that names their problem, a one-line outcome, and 3 short trust signals. Plain, warm, no hype, no jargon.

How to use it

  1. Write the words first (second prompt) if the blank page is hard.
  2. Build the page (first prompt), paste your live app link into the button.
  3. Test the button on a phone - it must open your working app.
  4. Live beats perfect: ship the link today, polish later.
The site has ONE job: make a stranger act. The clearer the offer, the more people tap. Cut anything that is not helping them act.
