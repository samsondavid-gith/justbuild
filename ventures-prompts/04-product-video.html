Show it working.

Session 5 · 60 min  |  Model: a light copy model for the script + a free screen recorder

A walkthrough is you, showing your thing actually working. 60-90 seconds. Proof beats claims. Write the script, then record the flow you already tested.

Prompt - write the 60-90 second script
Write me a 60 to 90 second screen-recording script for a walkthrough of my product [my business]. It is me, on my phone, showing my live app working. Arc: 1. Hook (first 3 seconds): name the problem [their pain]. 2. Show the app: browse the catalog, pick and configure one item, "pay" (demo), get an order number, track it. Narrate what I am doing in my own warm voice. 3. Close: one clear next step [e.g. "try it, link below"]. Give me the exact words to say and, in brackets, what to show on screen at each line. Keep sentences short and easy to say out loud.

How to use it

  1. Get the script, read it aloud once, tweak anything that feels unnatural to say.
  2. Close every other app, silence notifications, open your live app.
  3. Record the exact flow you tested. Go slow, pause between steps.
  4. One good take beats ten perfect ones. Finish with the one next step.
Record once, use it everywhere - website, pitch, WhatsApp, demo day. A working demo is the best salesman you have.
