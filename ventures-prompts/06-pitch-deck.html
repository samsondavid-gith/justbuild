Your story in ten slides.

Session 7 · 90 min  |  Model: a narrative model for the content + a free AI deck tool (e.g. Gamma free tier) to render it

A pitch deck is your story in ten slides, building to ONE ask. You already have everything - the problem, the app, the proof. Assemble it, do not start blank.

Prompt - write the 10-slide deck content
Write the content for a 10-slide startup pitch deck for [my business], in the standard YC order. For each slide give a short title and 1-2 lines of content, big words, few words: 1. Problem (open with the pain, a named person) 2. Solution (my app, in one line) 3. How it works (the catalog -> configure -> pay -> track flow) 4. Product (a line pointing to my LIVE app + video) 5. Market (who and how many, locally, honestly) 6. Why now 7. Traction / proof (anything real: users, orders, my by-hand test) 8. Business model (how the first rupee comes in) 9. Team (me, and my unfair advantage / way in) 10. The Ask (one clear thing I want) Plain and confident. I will do the talking; the slides just carry the key words.
Prompt - turn it into a real deck
Turn this content into a clean 10-slide deck: one thought per slide, big words few words, one accent colour, consistent font. Content: [paste the 10 slides].

How to use it

  1. Run the first prompt to get your 10 slides of content.
  2. Paste it into a free AI deck tool to render a clean deck.
  3. Replace any mockup with a link/screenshot of your live app - the proof slide wins the room.
  4. Practise the one ask. The whole deck builds to it.
This deck outlives demo day - use it for real conversations. Ten slides, one story, one ask.
